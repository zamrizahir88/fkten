Welcome to FKTEN

Educating engineers for a sustainable, technology-driven future

FKTEN offers PhD, Master's, Bachelor's and Diploma programmes in electronic engineering, engineering technology and technology. Every programme is built around industry demand and the requirements of professional regulatory bodies.

"We foster future engineers and technologists with the skills and experience needed to succeed in a complex, technology-driven, global society."
  • Programmes accredited by EAC, ETAC and MQA
  • Industry-partnered labs with Siemens and FESTO
  • Full-time and part-time research degrees
