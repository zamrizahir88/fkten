Programme Overview

Broad, versatile and industry-ready

The Bachelor of Electronic Engineering with Honours is FKTEN's flagship undergraduate programme. It provides a comprehensive foundation in electronic engineering theory and practice, from circuit theory and electronic devices to integrated circuit design, communication systems and digital signal processing.

Accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Council (EAC) and aligned with the Washington Accord, the programme prepares graduates for professional engineering registration. Strong research and industry links enrich learning throughout all four years.

EAC-accredited and aligned with the Washington Accord
Specialised courses in VLSI, SoC, MEMS and analogue IC design
Industrial training placement after Year 3

Programme Outcomes

What you will be able to do

Aligned with EAC and Washington Accord graduate attributes.

PO1

Engineering Knowledge

Apply mathematics, natural science, computing and engineering fundamentals to develop solutions to complex engineering problems.

PO2

Problem Analysis

Identify, formulate and analyse complex engineering problems, with holistic consideration for sustainable development.

PO3

Design / Development of Solutions

Design creative solutions considering public health and safety, whole-life cost, net zero carbon, and cultural, societal and environmental factors.

PO4

Investigation

Investigate complex problems using research methods, design of experiments, and analysis and interpretation of data.

PO5

Tool Usage

Create, select and apply modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modelling, while recognising their limitations.

PO6

The Engineer and the World

Evaluate sustainable development impacts on society, the economy, health and safety, legal frameworks and the environment.

PO7

Ethics

Apply ethical principles and professional norms, follow relevant laws, and understand the need for diversity and inclusion.

PO8

Individual & Collaborative Teamwork

Work effectively as an individual, team member or leader in diverse, multidisciplinary, face-to-face and remote settings.

PO9

Communication

Communicate complex engineering activities effectively and inclusively through reports, design documentation and presentations.

PO10

Project Management

Apply engineering management principles and economic decision-making to manage projects in multidisciplinary environments.

PO11

Lifelong Learning

Learn independently, adapt to new and emerging technologies and think critically about technological change.

Career Prospects

Where graduates go

Graduates are eligible to register as Graduate Engineers with the Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM) and progress to Professional Engineer (Ir.) status.

Electronics Design EngineerIC Design EngineerEmbedded Systems EngineerRF / Microwave EngineerCommunication Systems EngineerSignal Processing EngineerControl Systems EngineerTest & Verification EngineerSystems Integration EngineerR&D EngineerConsultant EngineerAcademic / Researcher

Graduates work across Malaysia's electronics ecosystem: semiconductor firms, defence and aerospace, telecommunications, utilities, automotive and consumer electronics, and engineering consultancies. Many go on to postgraduate study in Malaysia and abroad.

Entry Requirements

Who can apply

STPM / A-Level

Minimum Grade C in Mathematics and Physics.

Diploma

CGPA of 2.50 or higher in Electronic or Electrical Engineering.

Matriculation / Foundation

Minimum CGPA of 2.00 with Grade C in Mathematics and one science subject.
