Dean's Message

Welcome to FKTEN

Thank you for your interest in the Faculty of Electronic Engineering & Technology at Universiti Malaysia Perlis.

As the world goes through the Fourth Industrial Revolution, technology and human capability are converging to bring unprecedented change to industry and manufacturing. Big data analytics, the Internet of Things, cyber-physical systems, artificial intelligence, wireless technologies and 5G networks are among the driving forces. That is why the lecturers and staff of FKTEN continue to find ways to provide quality education that meets industry needs and students' expectations.

As Dean, I am determined to build on our well-established position as one of the leading centres of education and research. We pay close attention to industry trends and to our students' need for real-world experience. We set high expectations, create engaging curricula and learning environments, and develop future engineers and technologists with the skills to succeed in a complex, technology-driven, global society.

I invite you to explore our website and discover the many exciting programmes available at FKTEN.

Faculty Background

Engineering, engineering technology and technology under one roof

FKTEN offers Doctor of Philosophy, Master's, Bachelor's and Diploma programmes. Engineering programmes cover Electronic, Microelectronic and Biomedical Electronic Engineering. Engineering technology programmes cover Electronic System, Electronic Network Design and Electronic Telecommunication Design, and the technology stream offers Industrial Electronic Automation.

All degree programmes are designed around industry demand and meet the requirements of professional regulatory bodies.

Well-equipped labs

Programming, microprocessors, communications, antennas, embedded systems, networks and bioinstrumentation.

Research degrees

Full-time and part-time research-based PhD and Master's programmes.

Global talent

Postgraduates from around the world on industry- and government-funded projects.

Industry relevance

Research that is competitive at regional, national and international levels.

Objectives

Our faculty objectives

01

Quality academic programmes

To offer and coordinate quality academic programmes in line with the UniMAP Strategic Plan.

Menawarkan dan memperkasakan program akademik berkualiti selaras dengan Pelan Strategik UniMAP.

02

Image and visibility

To coordinate and strengthen the image, visibility and sustainability of academic programmes.

Menyelaras serta memastikan imej, keterlihatan dan kelestarian program akademik.

03

Industry & community networks

To strengthen the faculty's network with industry and the community for mutual benefit.

Memperkukuhkan jalinan kerjasama fakulti dengan industri dan komuniti untuk faedah bersama.

04

Staff professional development

To provide a conducive ecosystem that promotes professional development among faculty staff.

Menyediakan ekosistem yang kondusif bagi menyokong pembangunan profesional staf fakulti.

05

High-quality research

To develop and strengthen high-quality research in engineering and technology that advances the electronics industry.

Memperkembangkan dan memperkukuhkan penyelidikan berkualiti tinggi dalam bidang kejuruteraan dan teknologi yang mendukung industri elektronik.

06

Holistic graduates

To empower holistic graduates who contribute to national development and advancement in the electronics industry.

Memperkasakan graduan bercirikan holistik agar menyumbang kepada pembangunan dan kemajuan negara dalam industri elektronik.
